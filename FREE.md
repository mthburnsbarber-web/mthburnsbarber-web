# Free Public Resources

These are public, free-to-use tools and templates from Michael Barber and Black
Label Tech.

## Utilities

- [Discoverability Evidence Kit](https://github.com/mthburnsbarber-web/blacklabel-discoverability-evidence-kit)
  validates public organization facts, evidence claims, and AI-search prompt
  panels.
- [Brand Leak Guard](https://github.com/mthburnsbarber-web/brand-leak-guard)
  fails a deploy when a built site mentions a brand it should not.
- [CDHash Verify](https://github.com/mthburnsbarber-web/cdhash-verify)
  compares the running macOS app to the candidate artifact you built.
- [DKIM Doctor](https://github.com/mthburnsbarber-web/dkim-doctor)
  checks SPF, DKIM, and DMARC DNS health for sending domains.
